Privacy Policy – Werewolf Survivors

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Werewolf Survivors ("the Game") is developed and published by 301 Games ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use the Game.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly request or maintain a separate database of personal information such as your name, postal address, or email address through the Game.

However, third-party services integrated into the Game may automatically collect or process certain information, depending on your device, settings, and location.

This information may include:

  • Device and operating system information
  • IP address
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Advertising interactions and app usage information
  • Diagnostic, technical, and crash information
  • Information necessary to process in-app purchases

2. Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob and its partners may collect and process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device information, and interactions with advertisements. This information may be used to deliver, measure, personalize (where permitted), and improve advertising services, as well as for fraud prevention.

The Game may display:

  • Interstitial advertisements
  • Rewarded advertisements

Advertising personalization and consent options may depend on your location, applicable laws, and device settings. Where required, users are provided with relevant consent choices.

For more information about Google's data practices, please visit: Google Privacy Policy .

3. In-App Purchases

The Game offers an optional one-time in-app purchase through Google Play Billing that permanently removes certain advertisements from the Game.

Payments and purchase processing are handled by Google Play. We do not directly collect or store your payment card information. Google may process purchase-related information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

4. Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: Used to provide and manage advertisements.
  • Google Play Billing: Used to process and manage in-app purchases.

These services may collect and process information according to their respective privacy policies.

5. How Information Is Used

Information processed through third-party services may be used to:

  • Provide and display advertisements
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Process and manage in-app purchases
  • Maintain and improve the Game
  • Detect technical problems and prevent fraud
  • Improve the security and reliability of the Game

We do not sell personal information directly to third parties.

6. Children's Privacy

The Game is not designed for or directed at children under the age of 13. Its declared target audience on Google Play is users aged 13 and older.

We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children through the Game. Third-party services may process information as described in this Privacy Policy and in accordance with applicable laws.

If you believe that information has been collected from a child in a manner that is inconsistent with applicable privacy laws, please contact us.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed in connection with the Game. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Data Retention

We do not maintain a separate database of personal information collected directly through the Game. Information collected by third-party services is retained and processed according to their respective retention policies.

9. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information.

You may also have options to manage advertising preferences, reset or limit your advertising identifier, and manage privacy or consent settings through your device or the options provided by Google.

You can stop using the Game and uninstall it at any time.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the Game, you can contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Game's Google Play Store listing.
